Welcome to the VR Tour!

Here is a quick guide on how to navigate the 360° environment:

Top Right Controls

  • Auto-Rotate: Slowly spins the camera automatically.
  • Fullscreen: Expands the tour to fill your entire screen.
  • Locations Panel: Opens a sidebar to travel to different rooms and their sections.

Hotspots

  • Link Hotspots: Click these to move to a new area.
  • i
    Info Hotspots: Hover over these to learn more about the equipment or space.

Navigating the View

Click and drag your mouse (or swipe on touch screens) to look around.
